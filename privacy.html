Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the Kumo Chrome extension and this website handle information. Kumo helps users read supported task pages, prepare form drafts, keep notes and build email templates. The Kumo developer provides support at tezitokz@gmail.com.

What Kumo collects and processes

Task and form content
On supported task and administration pages, Kumo reads task identifiers, titles, page addresses, campaign and segment identifiers, dates, languages, content fields and related links to parse tasks and prepare forms. Page integrations can run when supported pages open; source imports and form filling require your action.
Drafts and preferences
Notes, task links, draft status, parsed task fields, settings, custom templates, configuration packages, language documents and email HTML/source text are stored to restore your work. Profile details you enter, including a name or avatar, are stored in the browser.
Files and screenshots
Kumo reads files you select or paste, including configuration archives, text, HTML and images. Email screenshot recognition runs locally using a bundled OCR engine. OCR screenshots are not saved with email drafts. A report screenshot is attached only when you select, paste or capture it; screen capture uses the browser picker and stops after a single frame.
Source documents and remote assets
When you import sources, Kumo retrieves the selected or linked Google Docs/Drive content using your existing browser access. Parsed text and prepared assets can be stored with your draft. Email previews and configured assets may load remote images or timers from the URLs in your content.
Optional bug reports
A report contains your entered name and description; task key/link; page URL when available; module, languages, Kumo version, report ID, creation time and diagnostic context. Email reports additionally include source text, HTML and a restorable editor snapshot for all prepared languages, including project details and embedded links/image URLs. Your selected screenshot is included if attached. We do not automatically redact report content.

Why the information is used

Task content is used to display structured task information and assist with form filling. Drafts and settings support recovery and your selected workflow. Email content is used to assemble, preview and export your templates. Bug reports are used to reproduce problems, communicate about them and fix Kumo.

Kumo does not automatically submit or schedule completed campaigns. You review and complete those actions in the destination service. Kumo does not sell data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or build unrelated browsing profiles. The extension does not include an analytics or advertising SDK.

Where data goes and who receives it

Slack — Kumo Test
After you consent and send a report, the configured Slack workflow receives the report fields: your name, description, task/page links, module, languages, version, report ID, time, filename or file link when present, and diagnostics. Email source text, HTML, editor snapshots and screenshot files are excluded from the Slack payload. Content you put in the description is still included. Slack processes this data and people with access to the destination workflow, list or channel can read it.
Telegram — support bot destination
The configured Telegram bot sends the report to its support chat. Telegram receives report details and a combined HTML attachment containing the selected screenshot and, for Email reports, source text, HTML and the restorable snapshot. People with access to this chat can view or download these artifacts. Support reports are not end-to-end encrypted chats.
Your task and administration services
Kumo reads and fills the supported pages you use. Entered fields are available to the destination website and its operator; the website may process field changes before final submission under its own rules. Kumo does not use a separate remote database for your draft workspace.
Google and Chrome Sync
Google Docs/Drive receives requests for source documents and assets you import. Site profiles and field mappings use Chrome sync storage and may be synchronized through Google according to your browser settings. Drafts, email documents and the saved reporter name use local extension storage.
Image and link hosts
Remote email images, timers and configured assets contact their respective hosts when loaded; those hosts receive the requested URL and ordinary network information such as IP address. Following a link or downloading a configuration contacts that link's service. The host depends on the content/configuration you choose.
This site and email support
This static site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub handles website requests and associated technical information, including IP addresses, under its privacy policy. This site adds no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies. If you contact tezitokz@gmail.com, Google/Gmail and the Kumo support mailbox receive your message, email address and attachments.

Service providers operate under their own privacy policies and may process data in countries other than yours: Slack, Telegram, Google, and GitHub.

Your choice before sending a report

Reporting is optional. The report form explains the data and recipients and requires an unchecked-by-default consent box before you can send. Consent applies to that report only and resets when you reopen the form. Cancelling the form does not send a report and does not prevent use of other Kumo features.

Review the task, description and prepared email languages before sending; remove confidential or unnecessary information and omit or remove the optional screenshot. Do not include passwords, access tokens or private customer data. Saving your reporter name stores it locally for convenience; it does not authorize future reports. Each outgoing report includes the disclosure version you accepted.

Storage, retention and deletion

In the browser: drafts and settings remain until you delete or replace them or clear the extension's data. Uninstalling removes local extension storage. Chrome-synced settings are managed through Chrome's sync controls. Downloaded backups, exports and configuration archives remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately.

Support destinations: submitted reports and attachments are stored in the configured Slack and Telegram destinations. Kumo has no automatic expiry or deletion mechanism for these reports. They may remain until manually deleted or removed under the destination service's retention settings. Support email follows the mailbox's retention settings. There is no fixed automatic deletion period promised by this policy.

To request access, correction or deletion of a support report, email tezitokz@gmail.com with its report ID, approximate date and reporter name. Do not resend the sensitive content. The developer can arrange removal of copies under their control; provider backups and any copies already downloaded by recipients may remain subject to their own retention rules. Uninstalling Kumo does not delete a submitted report. You can stop future reporting by not submitting further reports.

Security and browser access

Kumo uses HTTPS for configured report delivery and supported source imports. Data in local extension storage is protected by browser and device access controls; Kumo does not add application-level encryption to stored drafts. Use a trusted browser profile and protect access to your device and support destinations.

Browser permissions support local/sync storage, supported page access, tab context, the side panel, clipboard export, downloads, script injection and trusted interaction with form controls through Chrome's debugger API. The extension does not request Chrome's browsing-history permission or use its access to track unrelated browsing. Remote assets are retrieved from their supplied URLs. OCR and executable extension code are bundled with Kumo.

Limited Use and policy changes

Kumo's use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used and transferred only for the disclosed user-facing functionality and support purposes described here, not for advertising, sale or unrelated profiling.

This policy is updated when data handling changes. The date and revision on this page identify the current disclosure. Reporting changes are also disclosed in the report form before transmission.

Questions about your data? Contact the developer.
