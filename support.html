Help with Kumo, a bug report, or a question about your data.

Report a problem

Use Report a bug inside Kumo for a prepared Email or NC task. Review the data disclosure before sending: Slack receives the report fields; Telegram also receives the attached screenshot and, for Email, source text, HTML and a restorable snapshot for all prepared languages.

You can also email a short description, your Kumo and Chrome versions, and steps to reproduce the problem. Share only the information needed to explain the issue. Do not send passwords, access tokens or private customer data.

Manage saved templates

In the Email template library, open the actions menu and choose Trash to restore deleted templates or empty the trash. Removing a folder returns its templates to the main library. Local deletion does not remove downloaded exports or previously submitted support reports.

Request access or deletion

Email the address above with the report ID, approximate date and reporter name. Uninstalling Kumo clears local extension storage; it does not remove reports already sent to Slack, Telegram or email support, or files you downloaded.
